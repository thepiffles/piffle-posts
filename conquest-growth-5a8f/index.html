Which prediction method works best

  • Long averages beat last week's number. Week-to-week growth is noisy, so averaging many weeks usually wins. It should get better as weeks are added.
  • Smoothing across ranks helps. The growth at one exact rank jumps around depending on who sits there. Using the median of nearby ranks removes most of that.
  • Updates can't be predicted from the scores. No method sees an update coming. Knowing one is due is the only way to plan for it, which is why the forecast has an "if another update" column.
  • Players are harder to predict than cutoffs. A player's growth one week barely predicts their next week, so forecasts work at the cutoff level, not per player.
